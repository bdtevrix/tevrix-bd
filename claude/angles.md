## Confirm these first (they decide what every email may claim)

1. **Name.** The site's heading says **HireMind AI**; this repo, the Sheet and the signature say **HireMinds**. Pick one before anything sends.
2. **One-line promise.** The site's line is *"Hire smarter. In hours, not weeks."* Proposed plain version for emails: **"HireMinds runs the first-round interview for every applicant by voice and gives you a scored shortlist the same day."** Confirm or rewrite.
3. **Proof point.** The site has no customer, time-saved or accuracy figure (the dashboard numbers are sample data). Until there is a real one, emails make no performance claims at all. The 60-second proof video (checklist step 15) is the best stand-in.
4. **Fit for our segments.** Several features are developer-specific (GitHub analysis, live coding). Our targets mostly hire sales, customer service, hospitality and care staff. Confirm the voice interview and scorecard work well for those roles, and which languages it supports (UAE and Singapore will ask).
5. **Price.** The site shows a credit-based free trial (10 credits). The plan says $250/month with a free beta month. Emails offer only the free beta month; confirm that is what a prospect gets when they say yes.
6. **Candidate transparency.** Every angle below assumes HireMinds tells candidates up front that an AI runs the first round and that a person makes the decision. If that is not true yet, angle 3 is off the table.

## Angle 1 — Volume screening
- **For:** high-volume hirers (hospitality, retail, BPO, care, field sales) and SMEs with 5+ similar open roles.
- **Signal to open on:** the number and type of roles they have open now.
- **Context (do not quote as our result):** UK leisure and hospitality vacancies drew 150+ applications each in H1 2026 (Jobtrain client data).
- **What we may say:** every applicant gets a first-round voice interview, scored on the same rubric, with transcripts the team can check.
- **Never say:** "AI picks your hires", "screen out bad candidates automatically".
- **Ask:** free beta month on one of the open roles.
- **Sample (73 words):**
  > Hi Priya, I saw Northwind has six customer service roles open this week. Roles like that tend to pull in far more applicants than anyone can phone. HireMinds gives every applicant a short first-round voice interview, scores each one on the same criteria, and hands your team a shortlist with transcripts to check. We're offering a free beta month to a few teams. Would you try it on one of the six roles?

## Angle 2 — Speed to shortlist
- **For:** scaling SMEs (20–500 staff) hiring several roles at once.
- **Signal:** 3+ roles posted in the last 7 days.
- **Context:** a Totaljobs survey (900 HR leaders, Oct 2025) found hiring took about 8 weeks on average, up from just under 5 a year before.
- **What we may say:** first-round interviews happen as soon as candidates apply, at any hour, so the shortlist is ready the same day.
- **Never say:** "hire in hours", "cut time-to-hire by X%" (no proof yet).
- **Ask:** a 15-minute call.
- **Sample (67 words):**
  > Hi Tom, Acme has four sales roles live, which usually means a lot of first-round calls for whoever is hiring. HireMinds runs that first round by voice as candidates apply, at any hour, and scores everyone on the same rubric, so you start each morning with a ranked shortlist. Happy to show you in 15 minutes, or set it up free for a month on one role.
- approved: no

## Angle 3 — Fair, consistent and explainable first round
- **For:** Heads of People, HR Directors; companies with a visible DEI or employer-brand focus. Strongest in UK and Ireland.
- **Context:** Greenhouse's 2026 candidate survey found only 13% of UK and 9% of Irish candidates were clearly told when AI was used; the ICO expects meaningful human review of automated recruitment decisions.
- **What we may say:** the same questions and rubric for every candidate, full transcripts and audio, and a person makes every decision; technical failures go to manual review instead of counting against the candidate.
- **Never say:** "unbiased", "removes bias", "compliant" (we cannot certify that), anything about SOC 2 beyond "Type I targeted for 2026".
- **Ask:** a 15-minute call.
- **Sample (68 words):**
  > Hi Siobhán, I noticed Harbour is hiring a People Partner and three support roles. Many teams worry that AI screening is a black box for candidates. HireMinds does the opposite: every applicant gets the same structured voice interview and rubric, you get the full transcript and score breakdown, and a person on your team makes every decision. Would a 15-minute look be useful while those roles are open?
- approved: no

## Angle 4 — Recruiter bandwidth
- **For:** companies whose open roles include a recruiter, TA partner or HR manager (the high-intent signal).
- **Signal:** "you're hiring a recruiter".
- **What we may say:** the first-round calls a recruiter would spend most of the week on happen automatically; the new hire starts with shortlists.
- **Never say:** "you don't need that recruiter".
- **Ask:** free beta month.
- **Sample (62 words):**
  > Hi Raj, I see Kiwi Logistics is hiring a Talent Acquisition Partner. When they start, first-round screening calls will likely take most of their week. HireMinds runs those calls by voice and scores every candidate the same way, so your new recruiter starts with shortlists rather than a phone list. Want a free month on your open roles while you hire them?
- approved: no

## Angle 5 — Agency cost per placement
- **For:** recruitment and staffing agencies, especially volume desks (industrial, hospitality, customer service, care).
- **Context:** UK volume roles typically bill 12.5–15% of first-year salary (Recruitly 2026 guide), so consultant hours spent on first-round calls hit margin directly.
- **What we may say:** first-round voice interviews for every applicant, scored and transcribed, so consultants spend their time with clients and the best candidates; a scorecard can go to the client with the shortlist.
- **Never say:** a margin or placement-rate improvement figure.
- **Ask:** free beta month on one desk or one client's roles.
- **Sample (67 words):**
  > Hi Claire, Harbour Recruitment looks busy on hospitality roles in Auckland. On volume desks the first-round calls eat consultant time that is hard to bill for. HireMinds runs a short voice interview with every applicant, scores them on one rubric, and gives you transcripts you can share with the client alongside the shortlist. We're giving a few agencies a free beta month. Worth trying on one desk?
- approved: no

## How Claude picks an angle (once approved)
- Agency segment → angle 5.
- Hiring a recruiter / TA / HR role → angle 4.
- 5+ similar volume roles, or a volume industry → angle 1.
- Contact is HR / People leadership and nothing above applies → angle 3.
- Otherwise → angle 2.
- The Monday report compares reply rates by angle; after about 100 sends per angle, drop the weakest.
