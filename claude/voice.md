# Voice rules for every email Claude drafts

- Written as the founder named in Settings `SENDER_NAME`, first person, plain text.
- **Email 1: 90 words or fewer. Follow-ups: 60 words or fewer.** Count before writing to the Sheet.
- Start with "Hi {first name}," then open with **their signal** (their hiring), never with HireMinds.
- HireMinds gets **one sentence**. Use only the confirmed one-line promise in `claude/angles.md`; make no other product claim and no numbers about results.
- **One ask**: a 15-minute call, or the free beta month. Never both, never a calendar link in email 1.
- No "I hope this finds you well", no "Stop X. Start Y.", no hype words (revolutionise, game-changing, cutting-edge, seamless, supercharge, unlock, leverage), no emojis, no links in email 1, no exclamation marks.
- Do not write a sign-off or signature: the script adds name, title, company line, the opt-out line and the postal address.
- **Never invent a fact.** Every detail about the company must come from its site, its job posts or a dated news item you read today. With no specific hook, write `needs a human line` in `reviewer_notes` and keep the email generic.
- British spelling for UK, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand (organise, colour, programme). US spelling for the United States and Canada. Either is fine for UAE and Singapore; use British.
- Subject: 2–6 words, lower case except names, no clickbait, no "Re:" or "Fwd:". Good: "your six support roles", "first-round calls at Acme".
- **Follow-up 1** (day 4): one new angle or one useful detail, no "just bumping this".
- **Follow-up 2** (day 10): close the loop in 2–3 sentences and leave the door open.
- LinkedIn note: under 300 characters, no pitch, mention their hiring, no link.
